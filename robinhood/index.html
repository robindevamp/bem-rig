Guide

Start mining in 6 steps

Get a wallet

Use MetaMask, OKX, Binance Wallet or any compatible wallet. Switch to Robinhood Chain and keep a little ETH for gas.

Buy the token

Buy the official token on Flap using the Official CA above. Every buy and sell pays ETH tax into the vault, which is where all PONS comes from.

Connect

Tap Connect wallet at the top. Hash Farm reads your token balance, your rigs and your pending PONS.

Buy 5+ rigs

Pick a number and tap Burn token & buy rigs. The first time, your wallet asks you to approve the token. At 5 rigs they start spinning.

Watch PONS flow

Pending PONS grows every second. When new tax arrives, anyone can tap Harvest to swap it into PONS and top up the pool.

Claim

Tap Claim PONS whenever you like. Your rigs keep mining after you claim.

Are rigs related to the Pons launchpad?

No. Rigs are the vault's own miners, recorded in the contract. PONS comes from the vault buying PONS on Uniswap with tax ETH and sharing it among rig holders.

Why do I need at least 5 rigs?

That is the vault rule. Wallets under 5 rigs earn nothing. Once you reach 5, you earn from that moment on, with no back pay.

Where do the tokens I spend on rigs go?

They are sent to the burn address 0x…dEaD, which lowers the supply. Nobody can get them back.

Do rigs expire? Can I transfer them?

Rigs are permanent and tied to the wallet that bought them. They cannot be transferred. The more rigs there are, the smaller each rig's share.

Can the dev withdraw the vault?

No. The contract has no owner. The dev only receives the commission recommended by Flap, taken automatically at each harvest.
